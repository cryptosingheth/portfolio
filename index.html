The thesis

Agents are about to hold money. Everything I shaped this year assumes it, and in every case the guardrails are the product.

  • Vanna · MCP server

    An agent borrows against a margin account with a health factor it cannot talk its way past.

  • Auri · Agent rails, concept

    An agent buys, borrows or sends gold only inside limits its owner set on a policy contract.

  • Perflo · Spending policies

    Caps, allowlists and access windows checked before the money moves.

  • Nahar companies · Approval gates

    Agents draft the paperwork. A person signs.
