Vanna

Founder & Product Lead · 2023 — present

Credit that composes. Leverage that holds.

Crypto lets you borrow less than you put down, and locks that collateral inside one venue, so your credit cannot follow the trade.

  • Lend an account more than it holds and someone has to price every open position, live.
  • Liquidation is the product. If the account is wrong for one block, the lenders pay for it.
  • A venue you cannot see inside is not an integration. It is a hole in your solvency.
Conceived
One margin account that carries every position, so credit follows the trade instead of being stranded in the venue that issued it.
Owned
Led the entire product UI/UX, co-authored the economic-risk assessment with my cofounder, and wrote the agentic use cases the site publishes.
Shipped
Live on Stellar/Soroban testnet. MCP server and AI Copilot delivered. Mainnet beta next on a milestone grant.

Beyond the interface

  • Co-authored the economic-risk assessment: liquidation logic and the risk engine. My cofounder led the security audit.
  • Made the risk dashboard part of the product rather than a report, so bad debt and liquidation distance are public.
  • Wrote the agentic use cases on vanna.finance. MCP server and AI Copilot are delivered; Agent Score and Risk Guardian are designed, not built.
  • Built a full integration toward Hyperliquid and deep work toward Aster, then walked away from both on solvency grounds at the time.
  • 20+ ecosystem partnerships, of which 15+ are live integrations across 6+ chains. $145K Stellar Community Fund grant, two of three tranches disbursed.

Live on Stellar/Soroban testnet. Closed alpha: 130+ test wallets, 99 margin accounts. Mainnet beta next, with ecosystem-committed external audit credits.

Auri

Founder · 2026 — present

Gold has been sleeping for 5,000 years.

People who buy gold can hold it and little else. They cannot spend it, borrow against it, or send it home.

  • The machinery that makes it work is the part the customer must never see.
  • A money app is judged on trust before anyone uses it, and one wrong word is a regulatory problem.
  • Borrowing against gold can liquidate. That has to be visible without scaring the customer off.
Conceived
A gold neobank for people who will never say the word DeFi: buy real gold, borrow against it, send it, gift it.
Owned
Researched the market, chose every building block, designed all four surfaces and 27 screens, and built auri.money end to end.
Shipped
Pre-launch on Arbitrum mainnet in private testing, as a one-person company with contract engineering.

Beyond the interface

  • Wrote the regulatory copy rules the product and the marketing site both follow.
  • Built auri.money with proof figures read from on-chain gold supply, and a borrow calculator showing how far gold can fall.
  • A 12-page docs site with 13 flow diagrams and a search box.
  • Chose self-hosted support tooling because Canadian record-keeping rules require five years of history.

Pre-launch. Live on Arbitrum mainnet, in private testing. One person, with contract engineering and ecosystem partners. No user or revenue numbers.

ShieldLend

Personal project · 2026

Lending that does not leak the borrower.

Every on-chain loan is public, so a borrower's position, size and timing are readable by anyone who wants to trade against them.

  • Privacy and solvency pull in opposite directions: hide the position and nobody can verify the book.
Conceived
A privacy-native lending prototype where a position can be proven sound without being published.
Owned
Built on both EVM and Solana after completing a zero-knowledge development certification.
Shipped
Open-source prototype. Not a product, and not presented as one.

Prototype, public on GitHub.
