Where this is going

Agents are about to hold money. The products that win will be the ones whose guardrails are the product.

  1. 1
    Agents will hold money.

    Not metaphorically: margin accounts, bank accounts, gold. Every product I shaped this year assumes an agent is one of its users.

  2. 2
    The guardrails are the product.

    A health factor an agent cannot talk past; spending policies checked before money moves; a person who signs. That is the moat, not the model.

  3. 3
    Privacy becomes the bedrock.

    Enterprise and digital-asset products will have to prove things without revealing them. Product leaders who understand proofs will build them.

  • Vanna · MCP server

    An agent borrows against a margin account with a health factor it cannot talk its way past.

  • Auri · Agent rails, concept

    An agent buys, borrows or sends gold only inside limits its owner set on a policy contract.

  • Perflo · Spending policies

    Caps, allowlists and access windows checked before the money moves.

  • Nahar companies · Approval gates

    Agents draft the paperwork. A person signs.
