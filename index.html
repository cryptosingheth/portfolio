Auri

Founder (Jun 2026 – Present) — gold neobank for humans and AI agents, built solo

Pre-launch · Arbitrum mainnet, private testing

Jun 2026 – Present · Toronto, Canada · pre-launch

  • Auri is a gold neobank for humans and AI agents, built for a web2-native audience with DeFi invisible: buy real gold in seconds, borrow cash in your local currency against it, send it home, gift it, auto-invest, and add leverage (Boost, up to 5x, always risk-flagged). No gas, no seed phrases. Public stack: Tether Gold (XAUT, allocated bars attested by BDO Italia), Enso routing, Morpho Blue borrowing, ZeroDev smart accounts, Privy embedded wallets, Arbitrum One settlement.
  • Built solo, in collaboration with Offchain Labs (Arbitrum) and ZeroDev: end-to-end product definition across four surfaces (web app, native mobile, mobile web/PWA, tablet) plus the admin console — 27 screens and 21 flows, from onboarding and KYC to buy/sell, borrow/repay, withdraw, send/gift/remit, auto-invest, Boost, rewards, proof of reserves and support. Shipped V2.1 to V2.3 through ~10 build-review rounds.
  • Authored the V2.3 correctness set so the UI never asserts what is not true: no fabricated on-chain receipts, live price impact including sell-side spread, a real deposit QR, Enso attribution, units that follow the global picker, an accessible-contrast accent, and coming-soon rails disabled.
  • Specified for engineering: a 1,600+ line screen inventory, an 850+ line API sheet, and the logic/validation and DB sheet; wrote the money formatting, borrow policy, market/quote/reserves/Morpho API routes and rewards ledger in the Next.js monorepo, plus a prototype edit harness.
  • Designed and built the entire North-American web presence: the auri.money marketing site (15 versions in the first 5 days, then a global repositioning with live proof numbers, an interactive borrow calculator and scroll-driven demos recorded from the real app), a 12-page docs site with 13 flow diagrams and search, legal pages, a style guide and a 99-SVG brand kit. 46 deploys via GitHub Pages.
  • Wrote the regulatory and trust copy rules: never 'a bank', a non-custodial software platform, not CDIC-insured, liquidation-risk disclosure on every borrow and Boost surface, KYC and fiat via licensed partners, 'attested' not 'audited', and proof figures verified at source with as-of dates. KYC justified under Canadian FINTRAC rules; support design chose self-hosted Chatwoot plus a RAG bot on the MSB five-year record-retention requirement.
  • Vendor and data evaluations (support tooling, docs platform, wallet providers, gold-price API and reserves data) plus a feature-gap register against Nexo, Ledn, Arch, Coinbase and Aave. AI-native delivery: ran 8- and 17-agent build rounds with committed verification gates, and closed every build-review round with a written record.

Founder-led protocol

Vanna Protocol

Founder & Product Lead — on-chain prime broker & undercollateralized credit infrastructure

Stellar/Soroban testnet — closed alpha

Nov 2023 – Present

  • Founded and lead product for composable, undercollateralized credit infrastructure for DeFi: an on-chain prime broker for traders, businesses, institutions and AI agents. One margin account, borrow against mixed collateral, deploy across perps, options, spot, yield, prediction markets and tokenized RWAs. The core innovation is the Track Token, minted per margin account so the oracle can price every position live on-chain.
  • Live on Stellar/Soroban testnet: margin accounts with a single account-level health factor across all collateral and debt (isolated margin today; unified portfolio margin is the architecture direction), a stateless risk engine, utilization-priced rates and per-asset lending pools, integrations with Blend, Aquarius and Soroswap, the Reflector oracle, Pro (multi-leg) and Lite (one-click) modes, and a public economic risk-monitoring dashboard. Closed alpha: 130+ test wallets, 99 margin accounts. Heading to Stellar mainnet beta; EVM (Base/Arbitrum/OP) on the roadmap.
  • Own product, risk frameworks, tokenomics, GTM, technical docs and ecosystem partnerships (20+ chains, protocols and bridges); co-authored the internal economic-risk assessment; rebuilt the company AI-native (~33% lower build/ops cost); led the vanna.finance website redesign through 11 review rounds.
  • Funding: $350K pre-seed from Web3 funds and angels, a $145K Stellar Community Fund grant (milestone-based), and the Draper University / Stellar Founder Residency. Deliberately no token pre-PMF.
  • Agentic direction (roadmap): an intent Copilot with MCP/CLI rails near-ready; Agent Score and an autonomous Risk Guard designed, early.
  • Venue diligence: built a full integration toward Hyperliquid and deep integration work toward Aster, then walked away from both on solvency grounds at the time.
