app.vanna.finance/analytics
app.vanna.finance/margin
One health factor for the whole account.All collateral against all debt, one number, recomputed before every action. Nothing ships that cannot be expressed in it.
Publish the risk instead of describing it.The risk dashboard is public and shows bad debt, health factors and liquidation distance across all accounts. The solvency claim is checkable by anyone.
Say what is live and what is direction.Isolated margin with one account-level health factor is what exists. Unified portfolio margin is where it is going, and the site says so.

Auri · auri.money · Founder · 2026 — present

Gold has been sleeping for 5,000 years.

People who buy gold can hold it and little else. They cannot spend it, borrow against it, or send it home.

Beyond the interface

  • Wrote the regulatory copy rules the product and the marketing site both follow: never “a bank”, non-custodial, not CDIC-insured, attested not audited.
  • Built auri.money end to end, with proof figures read from on-chain gold supply and a borrow calculator that shows how far gold can fall.
  • Chose self-hosted support tooling because Canadian record-keeping rules require five years of history.

Pre-launch. Live on Arbitrum mainnet, in private testing. One person, with contract engineering and ecosystem partners Offchain Labs and ZeroDev.

app.auri.money
auri.money — proof of reserves
The app never shows a number that is not true.No transaction hash unless it exists on-chain. Price impact live, including the sell-side spread. Rails that are not live are switched off and labelled, not mocked.
Every on-chain function is a plain banking action.Buy. Add cash. Borrow. Send. The words wallet, gas and seed phrase never appear in the experience.
Liquidation risk appears where the risk is taken.Every borrow and Boost surface carries it. The leverage feature is the most cautious screen in the app.
